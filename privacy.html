OnSceneLaw privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

OnSceneLaw is published by Solar Peak Software (“we”). This policy describes how the Android app collects, uses, and stores personal information. The app is an operational field reference for Ontario peace officers. It is not directed at children under 18.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, and if you use Google Sign-In, the Google ID token needed to create that session.
  • Device lock: a hashed ANDROID_ID and a device model label so a paid login can run on two devices.
  • Purchases: Google Play purchase tokens and entitlement timestamps so Premium can be restored and kept available offline for at least 30 days after a verified check.
  • Feedback: messages you send (help, suggestions, error reports), optional contact email, and the card or dataset version you pointed at.
  • Admin grants: if we issue a timed Pro grant, the expiry is stored on your profile.

We do not use advertising IDs, do not show ads, and do not sell personal information.

Why we collect it

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Enforce the two-device limit and prevent password sharing.
  • Provide and restore the paid subscription.
  • Receive and review field-card feedback.
  • Operate and secure the service (PIPEDA-compatible purposes).

Where it is stored

Account, device, purchase, and feedback records are stored in Supabase, a cloud database processor acting on our instructions. Google processes Sign-In and Play Billing. Those processors may store data outside Canada. We use them only to run OnSceneLaw.

Retention

We keep this information while your account exists. Deleting the account removes your auth user and cascaded profile and device rows. Feedback you already sent may be retained in anonymized or admin-review form if it is needed to correct the catalog. Google Play keeps its own purchase records.

Your choices

  • Sign out from Settings.
  • Delete your OnSceneLaw account from Settings → Delete account. That does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel that in Google Play.
  • Email the address on the Google Play listing if you cannot use the in-app deletion control.

Contact

Solar Peak Software. Use the contact email on the OnSceneLaw Google Play listing, or solarpeaksoftware@gmail.com.
