OnSceneLaw privacy policy

Last updated: 01 November 2026

OnSceneLaw is published by Solar Peak Software (“we”). This policy describes how the Android app and web utility collect, use, and store personal information. The app is an operational field reference for Ontario peace officers. It is not directed at children under 18.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, and if you use Google Sign-In, the Google ID token needed to create that session.
  • Device lock: a hashed ANDROID_ID and a device model label so a paid login can run on two devices.
  • Purchases: Google Play purchase tokens, Stripe checkout session markers, and entitlement timestamps so Premium can be restored and kept available offline for at least 30 days after a verified check.
  • Feedback & Error Reports: messages you send (help, suggestions, and flagged error reports), optional contact email, and the card or dataset version you pointed at.
  • Admin grants: if we issue a timed Pro grant, the expiry is stored on your profile.

We do not use advertising IDs, do not show ads, and do not sell personal information.

Why we collect it

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Enforce the two-device limit and prevent unauthorized account sharing.
  • Provide and restore paid subscriptions.
  • Receive, route, and review field-card feedback and flagged error reports.
  • Operate and secure the service (PIPEDA-compatible purposes).

Where it is stored

Account, device, purchase, and feedback records are securely stored in Supabase, a cloud database processor acting on our instructions. Transactional system notifications, support messages, and flagged error reports are routed and delivered via Resend (Resend Inc.) as our email delivery processor. Google and Stripe process Sign-In, Play Billing, and web subscriptions. These processors may store data outside Canada and are used strictly to run OnSceneLaw.

Retention

We keep this information while your account exists. Deleting the account removes your auth user and cascaded profile and device rows. Feedback and error reports you already sent may be retained in anonymized or admin-review form if needed to correct or update the legal catalog. Google Play and Stripe keep their own billing and payment records.

Your choices

  • Sign out from Settings.
  • Delete your OnSceneLaw account from Settings → Delete account. (Note: This does not automatically cancel an active Google Play or Stripe subscription; cancel those directly through their respective platforms).
  • Email the address on the Google Play listing if you cannot use the in-app deletion control.

Contact

Solar Peak Software. Use the contact email on the OnSceneLaw Google Play listing, or solarpeaksoftware@gmail.com.

This app is a field aid, not official Crown or court text, and not legal advice.
