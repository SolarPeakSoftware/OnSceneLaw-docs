Field aid only

OnSceneLaw is an operational field reference for Canadian peace officers. It is not official Crown text, not a court or tribunal record, and not legal advice. Verify charges, set fines, ticket wordings, and case holdings against current federal and provincial/territorial legislation, official court schedules (such as OCJ schedules in Ontario), and the full judgment.

Jurisdiction scope: OnSceneLaw includes federal statutes (such as the Criminal Code, CDSA, YCJA, and Charter) and national field guides Canada-wide, plus provincial and territorial offence packs and speeding calculators across 9 provinces and all 3 territories. Quebec exception: Quebec includes federal statutes and national guides in English only; Quebec provincial statutes (such as the Code de la sécurité routière) and Quebec provincial fine calculators are not included.
