ONCA

R. v. Wills

1992 CanLII 2785 (ON CA) • Binding in Ontario

CanLII Reference Available

Operational Rule

An officer MAY search or take a sample on consent only after a real, informed choice: they know they can refuse, know what you will do, and know it can be used against them. An officer CANNOT treat silence, a nod, or submission to a uniform as consent.

Legal Threshold & 6 Mandatory Elements

  • 1. Express or implied consent given
  • 2. Giver had authority to consent
  • 3. Consent was voluntary
  • 4. Knowledge of the conduct requested
  • 5. Knowledge of the right to refuse
  • 6. Knowledge of potential criminal jeopardy
Occurrence Guide

IPV / Intimate Partner Violence

Step-by-step scene procedure and mandatory requirements

1
Immediate Scene Safety & Separation:

Separate involved parties visually and audibly immediately upon entry.

2
Dual-Complainant Assessment:

Determine primary aggressor vs. defensive actions. Avoid dual charges unless supported by distinct grounds.

3
Evidence & Victim Support:

Photograph physical injuries, document property damage, and issue mandatory victim service referral cards.

2. Secondary Caution

"You are not obliged to say anything, but anything you do say may be given in evidence."
